LEO Privacy Policy

Effective 16 November 2026. LEO is a voice and desktop assistant for Windows, made by Abhinawa. Contact: leosupport.official@gmail.com

Ringkasan (Bahasa Indonesia). LEO jalan di laptop kamu. Obrolan, ingatan, project, dan rekaman suara disimpan di laptop itu, bukan di server kami. Kami tidak punya server yang menyimpan datamu, tidak menjual data, dan tidak memasang pelacak atau iklan. Yang keluar dari laptop hanya yang perlu untuk fitur yang kamu pakai: pesanmu ke "otak" AI yang kamu pilih, dan data Google atau Spotify kalau kamu menyambungkannya sendiri. Kamu bisa memutus sambungan dan menghapus semuanya kapan saja.

1. What LEO is

LEO is an app you install on your own Windows computer. It listens for its wake word, answers by voice and in a chat window, and can do tasks on that computer with your approval. The developer does not run a server that receives your conversations: LEO talks directly from your computer to the services described below.

2. What stays on your computer

  • Conversations, chat history, memory notes, projects, tasks, routines and settings, in LEO's data folder (%APPDATA%\LEO).
  • Speech recognition for the wake word and voice commands runs locally (Vosk and Whisper models on your computer). Short voice clips are kept locally to improve wake-word accuracy and can be turned off in Settings.
  • Logs, which mask passwords, keys and codes before they are written.
  • Sign-in tokens and API keys, in the Windows Credential Manager, never in plain files.

4. Google user data (Calendar and Gmail)

These are off until you press Connect Google in Settings > Connections and approve them on Google's own page. You can approve only some of them.

  • Google Calendar (calendar.events): LEO reads your events to answer questions like "what's on tomorrow" and adds an event only after you approve it on screen. Guests are invited only when you ask.
  • Gmail (gmail.readonly): LEO reads and searches your mail to summarise it when you ask. It cannot delete mail or change labels.
  • Gmail drafts (gmail.compose): LEO writes a reply as a draft after you approve it. LEO never sends email; you read the draft and send it yourself from Gmail.

Google data is read on your computer, shown to you, and passed only to the AI provider you chose for that conversation, to answer your request. It is not stored by the developer, not used to train AI models by the developer, and not used for advertising.

LEO's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

5. Your choices

  • Disconnect Google or Spotify in Settings > Connections. LEO also asks Google to revoke its access.
  • Delete a conversation from its menu, memory notes in Settings > Memory, and turn off or open voice clips in Settings > Privacy & security. Deleting the folder %APPDATA%\LEO removes everything.
  • Uninstalling LEO removes the app. Your data folder stays until you delete it, so a reinstall keeps your history.
  • You can also remove LEO's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

6. Security

Tokens and keys are kept in the Windows Credential Manager. Actions that delete, send, pay or reach outside your computer ask for your approval first. Updates are signed, and LEO refuses updates that are not.

7. Children

LEO is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.

8. Changes

When this policy changes, the date above changes, and important changes are announced in LEO's update notes.
