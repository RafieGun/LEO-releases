LEO

A voice and desktop assistant for Windows. Say "Halo Leo", talk in Indonesian, and LEO answers out loud, opens apps, looks things up and does tasks on your laptop, asking you first before anything important.

Download for Windows

Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit. Free to install. The installer isn't code-signed yet, so Windows may say "Windows protected your PC": choose More info, then Run anyway.

What LEO does

Talk or typeWake it by voice, use Live Talk for a real-time conversation, or chat in the LEO Desktop window.
Works on your laptopOpens apps and sites, controls volume, brightness and media, reads and writes files in your projects.
Your choice of AIGemini (free with a Google AI Studio key), Claude, Codex or DeepSeek, each with your own account.
Asks before actingDeleting, sending, paying or reaching outside your laptop needs your yes, on screen or by voice.

Optional connections

  • Google Calendar and Gmail: ask what's on your schedule, add an event after you approve it, summarise your mail, and write a reply as a draft. LEO never sends email for you.
  • Spotify: play, pause and skip music by voice.

These stay off until you connect them in Settings > Connections, and you can disconnect them at any time.

Privacy

LEO runs on your computer. Conversations, memory and settings stay in %APPDATA%\LEO; wake-word and speech recognition run locally. There is no LEO server that stores your data, and no ads or tracking. Read the full privacy policy.

Updates

LEO checks for new versions twice a day and installs them while you're not using it. Every update is signed, and a version that fails to start is rolled back automatically.
