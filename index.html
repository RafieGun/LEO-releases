Quick commands

The everyday stuff skips the AI.

Short commands run straight on your laptop: about half a second, offline, and free. Anything longer or less clear goes to the AI you picked, which is told what LEO already did.

~0.5 sOfflineNo AI cost
  • Leo, buka SpotifySpotify opens.Quick, no AI
  • Volume 40Volume set to 40%.Quick, no AI
  • Baterai berapa?LEO says the battery level and whether it's charging.Quick, no AI
  • Kunci laptopThe laptop locks.Quick, no AI

Also: open sites and folders, brightness, media keys, screen off, time and date.

On your laptop

It does the work, not just the talking.

  • Opens and drives its own Edge browser.
  • Clicks and types in desktop apps.
  • Reads and writes files in your projects.
  • Codes in a project folder and runs its tests.
  • Works on your second monitor, so your main screen stays yours.
  • Controls volume, brightness, media and the screen.
Approvals

Your laptop, your yes.

Deleting, sending, paying, installing, or anything that leaves your laptop shows a card first. Answer by click or by voice.

  • No answer means no. After 45 seconds the card closes and nothing happens.
  • Dangerous needs “yakin”. A plain “ya” isn't enough for things like wiping a drive.
  • Money buttons stay yours. LEO never presses buy, pay, transfer or send by itself.

Optional connections

Connect only what you need.

Google Calendar, Gmail and Spotify stay off until you connect them, and you can disconnect them at any time.

Settings › Connections

  • Google Calendar

    Reads your schedule when you ask. Adds an event only after you approve it on screen.

    “Jadwal gue besok apa?”

  • Gmail

    Reads and summarises your mail. Writes replies as drafts after you approve. LEO never sends email; you send the draft yourself from Gmail.

  • Spotify

    Play, pause and skip by voice.

How LEO uses Google data

Calendar events and Gmail messages are read on your computer and shown to you to answer your request. They are passed only to the AI provider you chose for that conversation.

The developer does not store them, does not use them to train AI models and does not use them for advertising. Events are created only after your approval, and Gmail replies are saved as drafts, never sent.

Getting started

Up and running in three steps.

  1. Download and install

    Run LEO-Setup. No admin rights needed. About 1.5 GB on disk.

  2. Sign in with Google

    On first start. LEO also downloads its speech models, about 700 MB, once.

  3. Connect a brain

    Gemini, Claude, Codex or DeepSeek, with your own account or key.

Windows SmartScreen. The installer isn't code-signed yet, so Windows may say “Windows protected your PC”. If you downloaded it from this page, choose More info, then Run anyway.

Updates. LEO checks twice a day and installs new versions while you're not using it. Every update is signed, and a version that fails to start is rolled back automatically.
